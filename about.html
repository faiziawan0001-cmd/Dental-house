About Dr. Umar Liaqat

Dr. Liaqat has over 8 years of clinical experience and is committed to providing the absolute best in quality, thorough, and gentle dental care for every patient. He treats his patients like family and is exceptionally skilled in working with children, nervous patients, and complex procedures.

Dr. Liaqat finished his Bachelor of Dental Surgery (BDS) degree with high honors from a prestigious dental institution in Pakistan. He has since completed advanced specialized training in dental implants, endodontics (painless root canal treatment), and cosmetic smile makeovers. During his career, he has taken great pride in treating thousands of patients with care and precision.

Our Story

Where It All Began

Dental House was founded with a single mission: to make high-quality dental care accessible, comfortable, and truly painless for the people of Lahore. Born from Dr. Umar Liaqat's passion for transforming smiles and his dedication to patient welfare, Dental House was designed to be welcoming from the ground up.

Located on the first floor of Bilal Arcade in Johar Town, our clinic was built as a modern sanctuary — a place where patients feel respected, informed, and completely at ease. We invested in state-of-the-art equipment, rigorous sterilization standards, and a team trained in modern dentistry.

Today, with a 5.0-star Google rating and over 145 glowing reviews, Dental House stands as one of Lahore's most trusted dental practices. But we remain as devoted as ever to every patient who walks through our door.
