Root Canal Treatment (RCT)

Don't let fear hold you back from saving your tooth. Our painless root canal procedure removes infected pulp, relieves pain, and preserves your natural tooth — all in a comfortable, anxiety-free session.

Pain Relief Tooth Saving 1–2 Sessions Painless

What is RCT? Root Canal Treatment involves removing the infected or damaged pulp tissue from inside the tooth, cleaning and shaping the root canals, then filling and sealing them to prevent reinfection.

Why Dr. Umar's RCT is different: Using rotary endodontics and superior anesthesia, the procedure is virtually painless. Most patients report minimal discomfort even during the procedure itself.

Recovery: Most patients return to normal activities the same day. Post-procedure sensitivity, if any, typically resolves within 2–3 days.

Dental Implants

Replace missing teeth with permanent, natural-looking implants. Dr. Umar has successfully placed implants in complex cases — including patients requiring multiple implants after cyst removal — with pain-free recoveries.

Permanent Solution Natural Look Complex Cases Bone Preservation

What are Dental Implants? Titanium posts surgically placed into the jawbone, acting as artificial tooth roots. A crown is then placed on top, giving you a permanent tooth that looks, feels, and functions like a natural tooth.

Dr. Umar's implant experience: One of our most complex cases involved a patient with a cyst who required 4 implants simultaneously — all placed successfully with a remarkably smooth, pain-free recovery.

Benefits: Lifetime solution, no slipping, preserves jawbone, no impact on adjacent teeth.

Teeth Cleaning & Polishing

A professional clean goes far beyond what your toothbrush can achieve. Our ultrasonic scaling removes hardened tartar, polishing leaves your enamel sparkling, and the experience leaves you with fresh breath and a brighter smile.

Plaque Removal Whitening Effect Gum Health 30–45 min

What's included: Full mouth scaling using ultrasonic and hand instruments, polishing with prophylaxis paste, gum health assessment, and personalized oral hygiene advice.

How often? Every 6 months is recommended for optimal gum health and cavity prevention.
