4-Implant Restoration After Cyst Removal

One of our most complex cases involved a patient who presented with a significant cyst that had compromised multiple teeth. After careful extraction and cyst removal, Dr. Umar Liaqat placed four dental implants — restoring full chewing function and natural aesthetics.

The patient reported minimal discomfort throughout the process and a remarkably smooth recovery. Today, they enjoy a complete, natural-looking smile.
